Privacy Policy

Anti Matrix ("Anti Matrix", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, mobile applications, digital services, and Careers/Internship portal.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

By using the Anti Matrix website, mobile application, services, or Careers/Internship portal, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website and services, we may collect the following information:

A. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • First name & Last name
  • Email address & Phone number
  • Login credentials & Account-related data

Passwords are stored using secure cryptographic hashing algorithms and are never intentionally stored as readable plaintext.

B. Internship Application Information

When you apply for an internship via our Careers portal:

  • Personal details (Name, Email, Phone, Address, State, City, Pincode)
  • Education, Degree, Major/Specialization, Graduation year
  • Internship duration selected
  • Resume / Curriculum Vitae

C. Identification Documents

For opportunities where identification documents are required, we may collect:

  • Aadhaar document
  • PAN document, where applicable
  • College ID card, where applicable

Not every internship requires the same documents. Document requirements depend on duration and role. We do not request unnecessary documents.

D. Payment Information

Where an Application Fee is applicable, payment is processed via authorized third-party payment gateways. We receive transaction verification details:

  • Payment status & Transaction/Reference ID
  • Application ID, Amount paid, Date & Time

We do not intentionally store full card details, UPI PINs, or banking passwords on our servers.

2. Why We Collect Your Information

We collect and process your information for legitimate educational, operational, and business purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing candidate and client user accounts
  • Processing internship applications and generating unique Application IDs
  • Verifying application submissions and confirming Application Fee transactions
  • Reviewing candidate applications, academic background, and technical portfolios
  • Communicating with candidates regarding selection status and schedule updates
  • Generating official documentation, verification certificates, and Offer Letters
  • Maintaining candidate, student, and personnel records
  • Providing requested technology, development, and digital services
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access to our portals
  • Complying with applicable legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations

3. Internship Application and Selection

When you submit an internship application, your information may be accessed by authorized Anti Matrix personnel involved in application processing, candidate review, evaluation, selection, internship administration, and documentation.

Application Progression: Applied → Under Review → Shortlisted

Submitting an application does not guarantee selection. Your application status displayed in your account reflects the current live status maintained by Anti Matrix.

4. Application ID

After a successful application submission, a unique Application ID (e.g. AM-APP-XXXXXX) is generated. This ID is uniquely associated with your record and is used to identify your application during communication. You must keep your Application ID secure.

5. My Applications Section

Your user profile features a secure My Applications portal displaying your role, duration, submission date, fee status, and application status. We implement strict access controls to prevent candidates from accessing other candidates' records.

6. Identification Documents and Sensitive Information

Certain internship programs require identification documents (such as Aadhaar, PAN, or College ID) for onboarding, verification, and academic credential integrity. Where collected:

  • They are strictly used for legitimate verification and documentation purposes.
  • Candidates must upload only authentic, requested documents belonging to themselves. Do not upload unrelated personal records.
  • Access to document files is restricted to authorized administrative personnel via protected storage.

7. Resume and Educational Information

Your resume, educational credentials, and portfolio links are evaluated by our technical team to assess candidate fit. We do not guarantee that every submitted application will result in an internship placement.

9. Email Communication

We may send automated transactional and status emails to your registered email address, including application confirmations, status updates, shortlisting notices, Offer Letters, and program communications.

10. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information as a product. We may share data only with authorized service providers who support our platform infrastructure, such as:

  • Payment processing gateways
  • Email delivery services
  • Cloud hosting and secure database infrastructure
  • Security monitoring and fraud prevention

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, court order, governmental inquiry, or to protect our legal rights, property, and candidate safety.

11. Third-Party Services

When you interact with third-party service providers (e.g. payment processors), their terms and privacy policies govern their processing. We advise reviewing their respective policies.

12. Data Security

We enforce technical and organizational safeguards including authentication controls, password hashing, server-side authorization, encrypted transmissions (HTTPS/TLS), and restricted admin access.

13. Password Security

Users are responsible for keeping login credentials confidential. Anti Matrix will never request your account password via unsolicited emails or calls.

14. Data Retention

We retain personal data as long as reasonably needed for application processing, managing internship records, fulfilling legal/financial reporting, and resolving disputes.

15. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses essential session cookies and analytics to maintain secure logins, remember preferences, and improve responsiveness. You can adjust cookie settings in your browser.

16. Children's Privacy

Our services and internship programs are designed for eligible students and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without proper parental or institutional authorization.

17. User Responsibilities

You are strictly responsible for the accuracy of all submitted information. You must not:

  • Submit fraudulent data or forged identification documents
  • Upload another person's identification documents without proper authorization
  • Attempt unauthorized access to another user's profile or application records
  • Upload malicious scripts, viruses, or misuse application endpoints

Misuse may result in immediate application cancellation, account ban, and reporting to relevant authorities.

18 - 22. Your Rights, Corrections, Security & Policy Updates

18. Access to Your Information: You may request access to personal data maintained in connection with your account or application by contacting us.
19. Correction of Information: If any submitted application details are inaccurate, please notify Anti Matrix promptly with your Application ID for verification.
20. Data Deletion Requests: You may request account or data deletion, subject to applicable Indian statutory and financial record-keeping mandates.
21. Data Breach and Security Incidents: In the event of an identified security incident, Anti Matrix will assess the impact and implement remediation in accordance with applicable cybersecurity standards and regulations.
22. Changes to This Privacy Policy: We may update this policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or technology changes. Updates are marked with the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
